How connections are found

Shared records connect people. A connection search balances evidence strength with the number of introductions. You can inspect the evidence at each step to decide whether a path is useful.

Ecosystem distinguishes people in the same issue section, the same quarterly filing, and the same registrant during the same quarter. Relative route costs are 2, 3 and 4. A path through an exactly matched shared client has cost 6 and names that organization; registration-specific client IDs still require adjudicated organization matching before cross-firm paths become available. These costs express evidence specificity, not measured familiarity. Distinct shared quarters are displayed separately; duplicate issue pages do not add quarters and gaps do not establish continuous employment. Each registrant/client/quarter uses its latest uniquely posted filing, retaining earlier versions as source evidence.

Staffer routes use directory and payroll employment history by default. Detailed records take precedence; payroll trajectories fill uncovered intervals. Shared workplaces, overlapping dates, staff size and concurrent jobs determine exposure. Shared-employee roles contribute less exposure. Report-period windows remain labeled as modeled exposure. Ecosystem uses documented lobbying reports, official rosters and dated work evidence; each step states what the source establishes. Shared-report steps do not establish employment or personal familiarity.

One person record, one network

People whose latest recorded roles are internships are excluded from the network and connection paths. Former interns who later held other roles remain included; all original source evidence is retained.

Directory listings, payroll, lobbying reports and other records feed the same people view and network. A directory listing reports a role; payroll records show paid service during a reporting period. A person's Current or latest role uses the newest dated evidence in one consistent format. Matching records stay attached to that person, with all sources available in More source details.

Current as reported means a source explicitly describes the role as current on its date. Current status unconfirmed means the latest evidence records that job but does not establish that it continues today. Past roles and work associations keep their original meaning. These are evidence labels on one record, not separate networks.

The Staffer map overview shows the directory population. Payroll-enabled routing also includes historical person records. Every participant in a returned route is drawn over the overview, including historical people outside that browse population; the caption counts those additions. Selecting a person or finding a route highlights the selected connections. Circles group people for speed; they are not relationship edges. Search finds records without removing anyone from the map. Reset view clears the selection, search and person exclusions, and Focus route zooms to the chosen path.

People lists people. It holds payroll, lobbying, Quorum and Politico person records, plus a LittleSis person record once an identity decision has attached it to one of those; unattached LittleSis records stay reachable from the evidence pages instead. Offices, organisations and policy areas are not people and are listed under Offices & organisations beside the map, where each group shows the people whose own records name it. Current people are listed first: a source describes the role as current on its date, or the payroll trajectory is still marked current. Sharing a group is not a connection between the people in it.

How the people counts reconcile

Phone and email fields use the original Quorum staff exports where available. Their date is the workbook creation date, not an independent verification of current contact validity. Contacts are attached across sources only with an exact name or retained alias and a unique matching current member office. Missing contact details are shown as Not available; addresses and phone numbers are never guessed.

Staffer uses a bidirectional Dijkstra search over workplace exposure; Ecosystem searches from both ends over its evidence costs. Each continues until it proves the best route under the selected exclusions. It has no time or work cutoff. If the selected records contain no route, it finishes checking the relevant connections before reporting that result. Map distance and job seniority do not affect the result.

An office search compares currently listed directory staff. Where no current directory roster is available, it uses the latest payroll roster and labels its date. Former staff can be intermediaries. A source listing is current as reported; collection does not independently confirm ongoing employment.

Work records show connection points; they do not tell us whether two people know each other personally. Source records, dates and identity decisions remain available with each step and person.

Connection evidence and calculation

Staffer mode admits the directory population and eligible dated payroll employment. The Employment history selector offers a directory-only comparison. Unresolved Quorum matches remain separate, and internship employment does not create route connections.

Connection exposure accumulates over shared workplace intervals, weighted by workplace type, concurrent headcount and each person’s allocation across simultaneous jobs. Recognized shared-employee roles receive factors of 0.02 in the House and 0.05 in the Senate. The two people’s factors multiply. Duplicate rows do not add exposure.

Exposure becomes a saturating connection strength. Route costs penalize additional introductions, older evidence and reliance on one workplace. These are model assumptions, not measured friendship or success probabilities. Shared schools, topics, parties, undated service and lobbying co-reporting do not create Staffer employment ties. Person exclusions are shown in the toolbar and search form, apply only to that search, and can be removed. Different-people alternatives skip contacts from earlier choices; each alternative names those contacts. This does not create a permanent blacklist.

Source-only LittleSis and Quorum records remain fully browsable. They do not acquire cross-source identities or route edges without a supported match. Matching uses compatible names plus corroborating evidence; a name alone never combines people.
